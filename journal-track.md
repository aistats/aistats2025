## Journal-to-Conference Track information

We are excited to announce the launch of a pilot trial for the
Journal-to-Conference track at AISTATS 2025.

For its pilot trial, the track considers published papers from the *Annals of
Statistics*, *Biometrika*, the *Journal of the American Statistical Association*,
the *Journal of Machine Learning Research* and the *Journal of the Royal
Statistical Society Series B* for a poster presentation at the upcoming AISTATS
2025 conference.

- **Eligibility.** Eligible papers must:
    - Have been published no earlier than January 1st, 2023 in one of the following journals:
        - Annals of Statistics
        - Biometrika
        - Journal of the American Statistical Association
        - Journal of the Machine Learning Research
        - Journal of the Royal Statistical Society Series B
    - Not contain contributions that have already been published at a
      conference by the same authors. In other words, the journal paper cannot
      be an extension of a previous conference publication.

    Provided that the track is successful, this list of eligible journals will
    likely be expanded in subsequent years to include additional journals whose
    topics are relevant for the AISTATS community.

    <ins>For papers published in the Journal of Machine Learning Research
    only:</ins> Eligible papers must in addition not have already been presented at
    a prior edition of the NeurIPS/ICLR/ICML Journal-to-Conference track, as
    part of our efforts to coordinate with the NeurIPS/ICLR/ICML
    Journal-to-Conference track.

- **Applying to the Journal-to-Conference track.** Authors of an eligible
  published paper must submit their request through the following form:
  [https://forms.gle/YgJsvk9PGoy9CUfA8](https://forms.gle/YgJsvk9PGoy9CUfA8) \
  The deadline for receiving requests is **15 March 2025**. The deadline is firm. No
  extensions will be provided. You will receive the decision by email before the
  early registration deadline for AISTATS 2025 (23 March 2025). Accepted posters
  will also be announced on AISTATS website Journal-to-Conference track page.\
  We will proceed on a first-come first-served basis, unless the number of
  submissions exceeds the capacity at the venue for AISTATS, in which case we
  will prioritize early-career authors, make sure to promote diversity and give
  preference to researchers that do not already have a paper accepted at
  AISTATS 2025.


For more information, you can reach out to us at [aistats-journal-to-conference-track@googlegroups.com](mailto:aistats-journal-to-conference-track@googlegroups.com).

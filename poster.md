## Poster Instuctions

All posters must meet the following specifications:

**Portrait orientation**: vertical
**Maximum dimensions**: 60 inches × 36 inches (152.4 cm × 91.4 cm)


Your poster will be displayed on white foamboard standee size **78.7 inches × 39.4 inches (200 cm × 100 cm)** portrait. We will provide adhesive and means of hanging the posters.

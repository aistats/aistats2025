## Test of Time Award

AISTATS features a _Test of Time Award_ to recognize a paper from
10 years ago that has had a prominent impact in the field.
The AISTATS 2025 Test of Time Award goes to

**Chen-Yu Lee**,
**Saining Xie**,
**Patrick Gallagher**,
**Zhengyou Zhang**, and
**Zhuowen Tu**

for their paper

[Deeply supervised Nets](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v38/lee15a.html).

The paper was chosen out of the 126 papers presented at AISTATS 2015.
Congratulations!


## Best Paper

AISTATS features a _Best Paper Award_ to recognize an exceptional paper.
The AISTATS 2025 Best Paper Award goes to

**Charles Margossian** and
**Lawrence Saul**

for their paper

[Variational Inference in Location-Scale Families: Exact Recovery of the Mean and Correlation Matrix](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v258/margossian25a.html).

The paper was chosen out of the 583 papers presented at AISTATS 2025.
Congratulations!

## Best Student Paper

AISTATS features a _Best Student Paper Award_ to recognize an exceptional paper written by students.
The AISTATS 2025 Best Student Paper Award goes to

**Daniel Marks** and
**Dario Paccagnan**

for their paper

[Pick-to-Learn and Self-Certified Gaussian Process Approximations](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v258/marks25a.html).

The paper was chosen out of the 420 accepted papers written by students.
Congratulations!

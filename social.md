## Social Activities at AISTATS 2025

Unwind after your conference on Phuket Island with its diverse range of social activities.

**Staying Close By**: If you'd like to remain near the conference venue, the Splash Beach Resort provides a range of enjoyable activities. Take a break at their [water park](https://www.splashbeachresort.com/water-park), stroll along Mai Khao Beach, jog the scenic Beach Forest Nature Trail, indulge in a relaxing massage at a local spa, or seek some thrills at the [Phuket Wake Park](https://phuketwakepark.com/).

**Phuket city**: To experience more of Phuket beyond our conference location, the vibrant Phuket City Centre is a worthwhile destination. Please be aware that the car journey to the city centre from the venue typically takes around one hour. For transportation, we advise either renting a car for flexibility or using a ride-sharing service. [Grab](https://www.grab.com/th/en/) is the leading ride-hailing application in Thailand and is highly recommended.

During the conference, we highly recommend these two activities:

### Day 2: [Phuket Old Town's Night Market](https://www.hotels.com/go/thailand/phuket-walking-street)

- **Time**: Sundays from 4pm to 10pm
- **Price**: Free
- **Location**: Thalang Rd, Talad Yai, Muang, Phuket 83000, Thailand

 
### Day 3: [Siam Niramit Phuket Show](https://www.siamniramitphuket.com/)

- **Time**: 20:30 – 21:50  
- **Price**: Starting from 1800 THB (+400 THB for dinner)  
- **Transportation**: A transfer service from the conference venue to the show is available for 350 THB per person. Please arrange this directly with the company after booking your ticket. 


**Organization**: Social activities are self-organized by participants. If you'd like to coordinate an activity and need our help in advertising it, please complete this [form](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLScTb0eFe76IFZfu5itLAlywknkUQi-QByt9ZaLv2yZGYlzhAA/viewform).
